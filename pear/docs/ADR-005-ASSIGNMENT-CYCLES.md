# ADR 005 — Assignment plans, cycles and deterministic jobs

Status: implemented for internal course/award assignments and in-app notifications. External delivery and provider-specific recurrence semantics remain separate capabilities.

Plans are tenant-scoped and owned by an active admin/manager. Managers may assign only active direct reports; admin may assign active organization users. A preview validates the target, audience, UTC dates and due/recurrence rules. The human editor requires a current draft preview before save. Every write independently validates role, audience and target under library CAS. Admin editing another owner's plan explicitly adopts responsibility, with a required audited change reason.

Plans pin the selected published target version. Existing cycles retain their own target/rule snapshot and ledger. Future edits do not alter those snapshots or create another occurrence at the same scheduled timestamp. Course and award direct/self enrollment retains the previous first-enrollment behavior. Scheduled occurrences instead have unique cycle IDs derived from plan ID + scheduled UTC timestamp, and separate enrollment/attempt/evidence/certificate records. Awards in recurring cycles require course completion at or after the cycle start and evidence scoped to that root enrollment. A learner may enroll a course referenced by the enrolled award into that cycle; IDs/versions are derived from the authorized immutable rules, never a caller-supplied score/version. Referenced historical self-authored versions remain usable under the enrolled snapshot policy. Non-recurring/direct awards continue the prior credit reuse policy.

Fixed membership snapshots the authorized active audience when saved. Group changes do not silently change that cohort. Dynamic membership uses the current group and current account/direct-report authority for the latest cycle; joining creates a ledger once, leaving withdraws unfinished obligations, and rejoining resumes the same cycle/version/progress. Earlier cycles retain their original history. Inactive/out-of-scope recipients cannot receive new assignments; manager/target revocation blocks future dispatch. A group can be static or dynamic independently of the assignment's fixed/dynamic membership policy.

Dates use validated canonical UTC ISO timestamps. Human inputs are local-time datetime controls and convert to UTC. Recurrence is an integer cadence of 1–366 UTC days (0 means once), not a claim of calendar-month/DST wall-clock recurrence. Fixed deadlines apply to the scheduled cohort; recurring deadlines advance by that same cadence. Rolling deadlines use actual delivery time, including late jobs/dynamic joins. Optional endAt stops future delivery after the inclusive end. A one-time dynamic audience stays open to joins until its end/close. Close stops future cycles/joins without cancelling existing obligations. Cancel also disables unfinished cycle obligations and their child course writes while retaining progress, evidence, attempts and completed certificates; cancelled plans cannot be reactivated. New plans can replace them explicitly.

The scheduler is a deterministic server job, with no model or agent loop. It runs every 30 seconds while Pear is running; due work survives restarts. Each transaction processes at most 20 plans and five catch-up cycles; a durable cursor rotates plan reconciliation so more than 20 live plans do not starve. Clock injection exists only in trusted code/tests. The admin tool uses server time and takes no arbitrary time argument. Jobs atomically update cycle/delivery/enrollment, learner revision, notification and audit; unique constraints deduplicate retries. Background changes advance the library revision; a no-work rotation does not manufacture learner notifications. A failing audit rolls back the scheduler cursor and all learning side effects. Unknown results are reconciled using the same operation key or durable cycle IDs.

In-app assignment/completion/withdrawal/reactivation/cancellation notifications are private to each learner and unique by cycle + learner + kind. Completion notices derive from committed backend completion, including historical cycle reconciliation. Reading a notification does not mark learning complete. No email, Slack or Teams installation/delivery is activated; real connectors require their own authorization/evidence.

Migration 005 rebuilds enrollment tables once, under an atomic transaction with foreign keys temporarily disabled only for the table replacement, then validates all FK relationships before commit and reenables enforcement. Old records retain IDs, versions, attempts, evidence/certificates, with null cycle ID and active obligation state. Existing constraints become partial unique indexes for direct and cycle enrollment. No records are deleted for cancellation or re-certification.

Evidence: assignments.test.ts covers future/due rules, recurrence, fixed/dynamic joins/leaves, live authority, editing, cancellation, scoped notifications, fair bounded catch-up, audit rollback, retries/restart, recurring award fresh proof and child-course cancellation, and v4 migration with real graded attempts/certificates/evidence. assignments.spec.ts covers mobile human review/save/run/retry, notification read, learner grading/completion notice, reload and preserved certificate after cancellation. Full phase/parity status remains in CAPABILITIES.md.
