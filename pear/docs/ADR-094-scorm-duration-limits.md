# ADR-094: Trusted sequencing duration clocks

The 2004 parser admits all four absolute/experienced attempt/activity limit attributes in a bounded nonnegative XML day/time profile: days/hours/minutes/integer or centisecond seconds, up to 3660 days, canonicalized to seconds. Calendar years/months, negative durations, weeks and finer fractions are refused explicitly rather than converted ambiguously. Validate every organization and collection definition; an inline limitConditions group replaces the whole referenced group. Zero duration/attempt count prevents first access. Untracked activities remain exempt.

A host clock ledger is embedded in the trusted sequencing snapshot. Attempt absolute time accumulates while the technical attempt is open, including suspension; experienced time measures server-authorized active exposure, excludes accepted suspension/human close, and stops at the launch/session deadline. This is an LMS measurement policy, not learner attendance or content-reported CMI time. Activity totals accumulate across technical attempts; a new technical attempt resets its attempt values, while an authorized domain retake starts a separate ledger. High-water timestamps prevent negative deltas and repeated credit after wall-clock rollback. Every node retains its original identity and path; parent clocks measure their own intervals, not a sum that double-counts nested activity exposure.

Original engine delivery rules still decide flow, choice, retry and rollup. Both UP.1 paths consult a narrow ephemeral callback for duration-configured activities. The callback uses original definitions and the trusted clock, honors known tracking state and authored calendar/count controls, and applies duration checks on suspension/resume and current-session reopen. Accepted writable CMI completion makes attempt progress known without navigating or granting proof. Known status survives reconstruction, unlike the upstream activity-state serialization. Ending a session remains possible; a duration limit controls further delivery and does not fabricate failure/score. A stale client continuation denied by the host rolls back CMI, reported time, sequencing, receipt, audit and proof. Lost-response receipt replay does not spend time twice and still requires live authority.

Pinned MIT scorm-again 3.4.5 adaptation `pear-limits-v3` retains logging/selection corrections and fixes the two absolute tracking getters/setters that aliased configured limits. The two UP.1 entry points call the integration callback with their respective boolean polarity. No private engine object traversal is required. Public activity fields retain actual durations; original attemptAbsoluteDurationLimit supplies read-only cmi.max_time_allowed. Exact ESM SHA-256 is `de7a085e997136ad200f52a2221c2fec17e457c0d6a869735f613219b2dae10e`. Checksum/cardinality verification accepts only pristine, known logging-only, selection-v2 or exact current bytes; all upgrade paths and idempotence are verified. MIT copyright/license notices remain. Known prior 3.4.5 snapshots without a marker or with selection-v2 remain readable for existing profiles; unknown markers are refused. A duration-enabled attempted snapshot missing its trusted ledger is rejected rather than resetting elapsed time.

Tests cover all three editions, absolute vs experienced suspend/resume, deliberately inflated reported session time, zero/untracked limits, collection/default replacement and unselected invalid definitions, manual close/reopen, clock rollback, all four limits across technical retries, audit rollback, exact receipts, real database/server reopen, capability exposure expiry and stale parent-duration navigation. Three built-browser duration profiles exercise save/close/resume, lost ACK/exact retry, authenticated next-SCO delivery and official root proof with host clock values independent of reported time.

References: [IMS Simple Sequencing Information and Behavior Model](https://www.imsglobal.org/simplesequencing/ssv1p0/imsss_infov1p0.html), SM.3/TM.1.2/UP.1; [ADL fourth-edition Testing Requirements](https://adlnet.gov/assets/uploads/SCORM_2004_4ED_v1_1_TR_20090814.pdf), limitConditions XML binding; pinned engine source/public activity and navigation APIs. This bounded tested profile does not establish exhaustive SCORM conformance, Rustici differential parity, commercial exports or production/platform readiness.
